# Ascend UI Theme Hero Section Image Prompts

This document contains image generation prompts for visualizing different UI theme concepts for the Ascend student social network platform. Each prompt is designed to create a hero section mockup that showcases the unique aesthetic and personality of each theme.

---

## 1. Campus Confidence Theme

### Image Generation Prompt

Create a warm, encouraging hero section design for "Ascend" - a student social network website. The design should embody a "Campus Confidence" aesthetic that feels like a supportive study group environment.

**Visual Style:**
- Warm, welcoming, confidence-building design
- Soft, non-intimidating interface elements
- Cozy study group atmosphere
- Encouraging and supportive visual language

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Soft coral (#FF6B6B)
- Secondary: Warm gold (#FFD93D)
- Accent: Sage green (#6BCF7F)
- Background: Cream (#FFF8E7)
- Surface: Warm white (#FFFEF7)
- Text: Warm dark gray (#2D3748)

**Layout Elements:**
- **Header Navigation**: Warm cream header with "Ascend" logo in coral, friendly navigation menu, and a warm gold "Join Our Community" button
- **Hero Content**: Left side with encouraging heading "Share Your Journey. Celebrate Every Win." in warm dark gray, supportive subheading about building confidence, and two buttons - coral "Start Sharing" and sage green "Explore Stories"
- **Hero Visual**: Right side showing student success stories, small win celebrations, supportive comments, and encouraging community interactions
- **Background**: Soft, organic shapes and gentle patterns in cream tones

**Typography:**
- Friendly, rounded sans-serif fonts
- Approachable and readable text styling
- Emphasis on encouraging language

**UI Elements to Include:**
- Student celebration cards with warm borders
- Supportive comment bubbles
- Achievement badges with friendly icons
- Gentle, rounded buttons with soft shadows
- Encouraging micro-interactions and animations

**Mood & Atmosphere:**
- Warm and welcoming
- Confidence-building and supportive
- Safe space for sharing
- Non-intimidating and friendly
- Study group camaraderie

**Technical Specifications:**
- Desktop hero section layout (1200px+ width)
- Soft, organic design elements
- Warm color temperature throughout
- Gentle shadows and rounded corners
- Encouraging visual hierarchy

**Avoid:**
- Cold or intimidating colors
- Sharp, aggressive design elements
- Overwhelming or busy layouts
- Corporate or formal aesthetics
- Competitive or comparison-based visuals
